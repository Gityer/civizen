## Hard rules for implementation

1. **Classify before use** — never mix funding lanes into one unrestricted pool.
2. **No in-app investment checkout** until a compliant offering structure exists.
3. **Interest forms only** for investor, donation, and institutional pathways until acceptance is authorized.
4. **No fixed public distribution formula** — do not publish investor, contributor, or founder percentage pools.
5. **Restricted funds stay restricted**.
6. **Software ledger ≠ legal books**.
7. **Do not calculate or pay** retired percentage-based investor, contributor, or founder shares as if they were policy.
8. **Crypto / token mechanisms remain Disabled** unless expressly adopted.
