# Privacy Notice

This notice says what the Civizen application (web at civizen.world and the Android app) stores about you, why, and
what you control. It is written from how the software works today; it is interim and awaits professional review.
It does not replace the Terms of Use.

## Who runs the service

Civizen is operated by the Mature Humanity initiative. Contact: the e-mail address shown on the Contact page.

## What is stored, and why

- **Account and profile.** Your sign-in e-mail or phone, username, name, photo, country and city (if you add them),
  language, and the voluntary profile you build (education, experience, skills, trainings, endorsements). Needed to
  run your account and show your public profile. You choose under Settings › Privacy what else other members see.
- **Verification.** If you ask for identity verification, the ID photo and face photo you upload are stored for the
  reviewer and the result (verified or not) is kept on your profile. They are not government identity checks.
- **Participation.** Matters, comments, proposals, ballots, receipts, agreements, knowledge resources and the
  notifications about them. Public counts of ballots are published; your individual choice is never shown. A closed
  consultation keeps anonymous records.
- **Messages and calls.** One-to-one messages are encrypted on your device with keys only you and the other person
  hold; Civizen stores the encrypted text and delivery data (who, when). Call setup data passes through Civizen;
  call audio and video travel between devices (through a relay when networks require it) and are not recorded.
- **Device data.** The Android app can use the camera and microphone for photos and calls, your location only when
  you ask it to fill in your city, fingerprint or face unlock only on your device, and notifications you opted into.
  The app checks civizen.world for updates.
- **Technical logs.** Standard server logs (IP address, time, requested page) kept for security and debugging.

## What we do not do

We do not sell personal data, do not show advertising, and do not use your data to train AI models. The built-in
assistant (Civi) answers from Civizen's own documents and, for some questions, from your own records read with your
own permissions; those requests are not kept beyond the conversation history you can delete.

## Sharing

Your public profile and public contributions are visible to other members, and some pages (consultations, public
documents, Jobs) to visitors. Data is otherwise shared only with providers that run the service (hosting, e-mail,
the AI provider for assistant answers) under their terms, or when the law requires it.

## Your controls

- Change or hide profile details under Settings › Privacy and Edit Profile.
- Download everything you put into Civizen as one file under Settings › Account.
- Delete your account under Settings › Account: open ballots are withdrawn, personal details are cleared, your name
  on contributions becomes "Deleted member", and closed civic records stay as anonymous records.
- Turn the e-mail digest, push notifications and messaging keys on or off under Settings.

## Retention

Account data is kept while the account exists and removed or anonymised when you delete it. Encrypted message
copies kept for delivery follow the limits you set under Settings › Messaging. Backups are kept for a limited time
for recovery. Public civic records (consultation outcomes, published proposals) are kept as part of the public record.

## Children

Civizen is not directed at children under 16 and does not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

This notice changes as the software changes; the version and date above tell you which one you are reading.
