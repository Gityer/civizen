# Project brief

Civizen — civic growth-score and marketplace app (React/Vite/Capacitor, Supabase).

Mobile-first UI with bottom primary nav and **arc secondary nav** on Home, Study, and Market.

Operational docs: `docs/04-operations/dev/AGENTS.md`.

**Planet first.** Civizen is built for Planet Earth and all World Citizens; no country is first. Existing national and local structures are integrated through mappings to one planet-wide model of functions, improving what exists (owner directive 2026-10-29; rule in `AGENTS.md` §3).
